🏠About Us



DosroGhar is a web application designed for rental property management.
It facilitates the interaction between property owners, tenants, and administrators in the context of renting accommodations.
Users can sign up as owners to list their properties, providing details such as location, amenities, and pricing.
Prospective tenants can browse available rental listings, search by location and cost, and initiate booking requests.
The admin role involves approving or rejecting property listings and managing bookings.

DosroGhar aims to streamline the rental process, enhancing communication and efficiency among property stakeholders. {% endblock %}
